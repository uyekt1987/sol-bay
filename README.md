SOL BAY — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Sol Bay on foot and by car: steal cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and work through 5 story missions for Raka, Mira and the Ombak gang. A HUD with a live minimap, combat, money and progression, and a save system round it out. Everything — city, characters, vehicles, names, story and art — is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move, mouse to look, E to enter/exit a car, F to punch, click to shoot.
